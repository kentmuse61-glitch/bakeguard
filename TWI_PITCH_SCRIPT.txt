BAKEGUARD — 3-MINUTE CEO PITCH · TWI NARRATION SCRIPT
======================================================
HOW TO USE: Record this on your phone (voice memo), segment by segment,
in YOUR natural Twi. This script is a guide — adjust any word to how you
normally say it. Send me the recording(s) and I will cut them to the
exact screen timings and rebuild the final video in your voice.

TIMING GUIDE (match each segment to the length shown — the video waits
for your audio, and I will fit it precisely):

------------------------------------------------------
SEGMENT 1 — INTRO CARD (~14s)
TWI:
Akwaaba! Ɛno ne BakeGuard — sika-karanteɛ adwuma a na ɛda so da biara
wɔ HOPE Special Bread: Mampong, Nkoranza ne Techiman. Minit a ɛdi hɔ
no mu, mebɛma wo hunu sɛn na ɛgyina hɔ de afeefoɔ no sika te. Yɛn!
ENGLISH:
Welcome! This is BakeGuard — the money-guarding system running every
day at HOPE Special Bread: Mampong, Nkoranza and Techiman. In the next
three minutes I will show you how it keeps a baker's money safe. Let's go!

------------------------------------------------------
SEGMENT 2 — DASHBOARD (~19s)
TWI:
Anansieɛ biara, driver a ɔtɛɛ a ɔtɛɛ anim, saa adwen no na wɔhunu:
ade a wɔakrɛnam, sika a ɛbra, kii a ɛda so, ne sɛ adwuma no mu yɛn
sɛnea. Ade a ɛyɛ pa — kɔ; ade a ɛyɛ nna — ɛkyerɛ wo hɔ pɛ pɛ.
ENGLISH:
Every morning, before the first driver leaves, this is the first screen:
what was baked, the cash on its way, credit outstanding, and how ready
the business is. If it's healthy — go; if it's a problem — it shows you
exactly where.

------------------------------------------------------
SEGMENT 3 — PRODUCTION (~18s)
TWI:
Adwuma no fi mu: ɔkoma na ɛkɔ mu, adwinnba na ɛda — session biara.
Adwinnba biara wɔ ne bo mu sika, nanso ɛhunu pɛ sɛnea ɛkɔsi sika a
ɛsɛ sɛ wopii anansieɛ no. Sɛ session bi atetoa adwinnba 200 a, ɛma
adwene — ɛsɛ sɛ wɔma adwene anansieɛ no mu.
ENGLISH:
The work starts inside: flour goes in, bread comes out — session by
session. Every loaf has its price, so it knows exactly what today is
worth collecting. If a session lands 200 loaves short, it raises a flag —
and a reason is required before the day closes.

------------------------------------------------------
SEGMENT 4 — DRIVERS (~22s)
TWI:
Driver ne hɔ a ɛka sika so. Ɛkyer w'aka pɛ: ɔdɔn a ɛkɔ, faa, kii ne
ɔdɔn a wɔabɔkyerɛɛ — nna ɛyɛ baako pɛ. Ɛdɔn no: adwinnba 132 atetoa —
ɛda adwene da a wɔakrɛn ne nɔba no, ansa na driver no ɔkɔ. Manager a
ɔhwehwɛ sika no, ɛfrɛ ne sika bio, cedi biara so.
ENGLISH:
Drivers are where money leaks. It shows clearly: the trip that went out,
cash, credit and returns — they must be one. This trip: 132 loaves short —
the alert fires the day the figures are entered, before the driver leaves.
When the Manager checks the money, it counts it again, cedi by cedi.

------------------------------------------------------
SEGMENT 5 — KIOSK DEBT (~15s)
TWI:
Kii a wɔma kiosk ɔdeefoɔ no — ɛyɛ ɔpɛɛpɛɛ ade, nanso ɛtɔ sika. Sika
biara a ɛda so, ɛwɔ ahoɔden no so, ne ne da. Anigyeɛ nso ɛhunu no —
ɛyɛ ade a ɛma adwene.
ENGLISH:
Credit given to kiosk sellers — it's a good thing, but it leaks money.
Every outstanding cedi is on the ledger, with its date. Discipline is
seen too — it's a signal.

------------------------------------------------------
SEGMENT 6 — STOCK (~13s)
TWI:
Wɔn a ɛkyekyɛ adwuma no — ɔkoma, gas, packaging — ɛkyerɛ w'aka da
biara. Techiman wɔ da 0.6 — ɛyɛ nna; ɛkyerɛw 'reorder' anigyeɛ ansa
na ɔkoma no da.
ENGLISH:
What holds up the work — flour, gas, packaging — it shows you the days.
Techiman is at 0.6 days — that's a problem; it has already written the
reorder before the flour is finished.

------------------------------------------------------
SEGMENT 7 — EXPANSION RADAR (~13s)
TWI:
Ansa na ɛkɔ anansieɛ a di hɔ: ɛfrɛ kuro a ɛda w'akyi biara ho ne
ne anigyeɛ, na ɛma wo ahoɔden sɛ ɔdɔn a ɛdi hɔ bɛkɔ hɔ biara —
ansa na sika ne adwuma no mu yɛn ɛma no.
ENGLISH:
Before it moves to tomorrow: it examines every neighbouring town and its
market, and advises where the next drop should go — only when money and
the state of the business allow it.

------------------------------------------------------
SEGMENT 8 — 10 PM BRIEFING (~15s)
TWI:
Anadwo a ɛtɔn 10:00, saa briefing no na ɛbra w'ahohow so. Adwuma,
ɔdɔn, ade a atetoa, adwene ne ahoɛden — ɛkyerɛw deɛ CEO na, ɛnni
sɛ accountant. Wɔka no, na woahu adwuma no pɛ.
ENGLISH:
At 10:00 PM, the briefing lands on your phone. Work, trips, what's
missing, alerts and advice — written for the CEO, not the accountant.
Read it, and you know the business exactly.

------------------------------------------------------
SEGMENT 9 — MESSAGE LOG / ENGINE (~11s)
TWI:
W'ase no: ade a ɛduruu ne ho. Ade biara a wɔka — adwuma, ɔdɔn,
packaging, nneɛma bo — ɛma nkyerɛkyerɛw a ɛyɛ papa, sɛ WhatsApp no
mu na ɛyɛ — COO a na ɔno nna da.
ENGLISH:
Behind it: a message engine. Every message sent — production, trips,
stock, price — gets a clear reply, exactly as on WhatsApp. A COO who
never sleeps.

------------------------------------------------------
SEGMENT 10 — CEO VIEW + CLOSE (~17s)
TWI:
Na w'ahoɔden no: adwen baako, nɔba mmiɔn — sika a atetoa, adwinnba
a ɛtɔ, ne ade a ɛbɛtɔ — woahu no pɛ pɛ. Ɛda so da biara wɔ mmerɛ
mmiɔn — Mampong, Nkoranza ne Techiman. Tiaa 'YES' — anigyeɛ yi bɛda
so wɔ wo bakery mu kwan a ɛdi hɔ. Ɛno ne BakeGuard — fi HOPE Special
Bread. Me da wo ase!
ENGLISH:
And your view: one screen, three numbers — money missing, bread lost,
what will be lost — you see it exactly. It runs every day in the three
towns — Mampong, Nkoranza and Techiman. Type 'YES' — this system will be
running in your bakery this coming week. This is BakeGuard — from HOPE
Special Bread. Thank you!
